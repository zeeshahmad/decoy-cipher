🦆 Decoy Cipher

Your secret goes in. A perfectly innocent-looking story comes out.

Brighter words carried more secret bits. Dim words were nearly forced by the language model.

Both sides need the same password and the same theme. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded. First use downloads a ~140 MB open-source language model (SmolLM2) and caches it.

A toy, not vetted cryptography. Source & how it works.
